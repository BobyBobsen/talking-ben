Plush Character Concept

Design Description

A cute, soft-looking beige dog sitting comfortably in a pink armchair. The design features floppy ears, round eyes, a large black nose, short arms and legs, and visible paw pads on the feet.

Plush Design Details

  • Soft beige/tan body
  • Large floppy ears
  • Round embroidered eyes
  • Large black nose
  • Short rounded arms and legs
  • Detailed paw pads on the feet
  • Pink armchair as the main setting

Concept

The goal is to translate Talking Ben's recognizable character design into a soft, rounded plush concept while keeping the simple look of the original character.
